# 22 — Universe Design

> The Nexus is not one planet. It is an infinite universe that grows with
> the player — from a workshop on a hillside to the center of the galaxy
> and beyond. Every world teaches real science through exploration and
> wonder. This document covers the full universe structure.

---

## Layer 1: Home World (Foundation → Builder)

27 biomes on a lush Earth-like planet. Connected overworld with town
square hub, paths between biomes, buildings with interiors, nature areas.

This is where every player starts. Counting butterflies, sorting colors,
building bridges. The fundamentals of everything.

See [02-WORLD_DESIGN.md](02-WORLD_DESIGN.md) for full biome list.

## Layer 2: Home Solar System (Builder → Innovator)

## Layer 3: Nearby Star Systems (Innovator)

FTL travel unlocks neighboring stars. Each has unique alien worlds:

### Alpha Centauri System

**Ocean World** — Underwater civilizations
- Single-cell life in thermal vents (biology origins)
- Coral reef megastructures (bio-engineering)
- Bioluminescent forests (photochemistry)
- Underwater cities (pressure physics, structural engineering)
- Alien food: cultivated algae, thermal vent cuisine
- Alien culture: memory stored in water currents

**Desert World** — Underground civilization
- Surface: extreme UV radiation, sand physics
- Underground: crystal-illuminated cavern cities
- Solar-powered technology (photovoltaics at scale)
- Alien food: desert fungi, cactus nectar, mineral supplements
- Alien culture: knowledge transmitted through sand painting and dance

### Proxima System

**Tidally Locked World** — Eternal day on one side, eternal night on the other
- Habitable twilight band between extremes
- Dual society: light-dwellers and dark-dwellers
- Teaches: planetary science, thermodynamics, adaptation, diplomacy
- Alien culture: two civilizations that must cooperate to survive

## Layer 4: Galactic Exploration (Innovator → Creator)

### Crystal World — Silicon-Based Life
- Living crystals that grow, think, and resonate
- Communication through light frequency modulation
- Architecture IS the life form — buildings are alive
- Teaches: crystallography, optics, alternative biochemistry
- Alien vehicles: light-propelled crystal shells
- Alien food: mineral solutions, resonance nutrition

### Jungle World — Mega-Scale Ecology
- Trees 10km tall. Insects the size of buses.
- Civilization lives entirely in the canopy
- Ground level: primordial life, visible evolution from single-cell to complex
- Teaches: evolution, ecology, structural biology, scale
- Alien culture: pure symbiosis — every being serves the ecosystem
- Alien food: living fruit that regrows as you eat it

### Ice World — Thermal Extremists
- Surface: -200°C methane seas
- Life thrives in thermal vent cities beneath the ice
- Heat is currency (thermal economics)
- Teaches: thermodynamics, extremophile biology, cryogenics
- Alien vehicles: heated burrowing craft
- Alien food: thermal-reactive compounds, temperature-dependent flavors

### Nebula Civilization — Beings of Energy
- No solid planet — they exist within a nebula
- Bodies are organized electromagnetic fields
- Communication is pure mathematics
- The most advanced civilization in the galaxy
- Teaches: EM theory, quantum mechanics, nature of consciousness
- A player who reaches here is doing real university-level physics

### Ring World — Artificial Megastructure
- Built by an ancient race (precursors to The Founders?)
- Rotating ring around a star — artificial gravity through rotation
- Contains preserved specimens of 1,000 extinct civilizations
- Teaches: mega-engineering, orbital mechanics, materials science, history
- The largest museum in the universe

### Binary System — Shared Atmosphere
- Two planets close enough to share atmospheric gases
- When orbits align, air flows between them
- Two civilizations meet for one season per orbital cycle
- Teaches: orbital mechanics, atmospheric science, cultural exchange, diplomacy

## Layer 5: Other Galaxies (Creator)

Wormhole network connects to:

### Andromeda — The Mirror Galaxy
- Similar physics, different solutions
- Alien civilizations solved the same problems differently
- Teaches: comparative science, alternative approaches, creativity

### Dwarf Galaxy — The Dying Light
- Stars going out one by one
- Civilization fighting entropy itself
- Teaches: thermodynamics, stellar physics, existential philosophy
- The most emotionally powerful location in the game

### Quasar Galaxy — Extreme Energy
- Civilization harnesses a supermassive black hole
- Teaches: general relativity, energy physics, Hawking radiation
- The most scientifically advanced content in the game

## Design Principles for Alien Worlds

1. **Every alien world teaches real science.** Crystal World = real
   crystallography. Ocean World = real marine biology. No fake science.

2. **Alien life is genuinely alien.** Not humans with funny foreheads.
   Silicon-based, electromagnetic, thermal — truly different biology.

3. **Alien cultures are complete.** Food, vehicles, art, language,
   architecture, social structure. Each civilization feels real.

4. **Single-cell to complex.** Some worlds show the full evolution
   spectrum — from thermal vent microbes to spacefaring civilizations.

5. **No evil aliens.** Different ≠ dangerous. Every civilization has
   knowledge to share. Diplomacy, not combat.

6. **The universe rewards curiosity.** The player who asks "what's out
   there?" always finds something worth discovering.
