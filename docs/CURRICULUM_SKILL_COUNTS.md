## Key Implications for Nexus Academy

### Content Scale
- **~11,400 discrete teachable skills** across all subjects and all tiers
- Each skill needs game mechanics, progression tracking, and spaced repetition
- At the IXL granularity level (which tracks sub-variations), the number could reach **15,000-20,000**

### Content Generation Pipeline
- Atlas needs to generate quests, puzzles, and world elements for each skill
- With 3-4 years of content cached ahead, and assuming ~5 content pieces per skill:
  **~57,000-70,000 content pieces** in the pipeline at any time

### Hourly Scope
- A child playing 1 hour/day from age 2 to 18 = ~5,840 hours
- Total curriculum hours (26,900) exceed available play hours
- This confirms the need for **adaptive pathing** — no player covers everything
- The game must prioritize based on interest, aptitude, and prerequisite chains

### Subject Weight in the Game World
- **Math + ELA** dominate (~46% of all skills) — these should be deeply woven into every biome
- **Sciences** (Physics + Chem + Bio + Earth Sci) = ~26% — the world's physical rules
- **Applied** (CS + Engineering) = ~12% — building and creating in the world
- **Humanities** (History + Econ + Philosophy + Art/Music) = ~18% — the world's culture and society
- **Frontier** (2%) — rare, exciting endgame content that rewards advanced players

### Source Reliability Notes
- IXL skill counts are the most granular available (tracked per exercise variation)
- Common Core/NGSS provide the structural framework (broader standards)
- OpenStax chapter counts validate college-level topic scope
- Khan Academy provides the best freely-available cross-subject skill mapping
- All numbers are approximations — the game will discover exact counts during content authoring
