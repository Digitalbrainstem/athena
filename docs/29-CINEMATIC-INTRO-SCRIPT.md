# Nexus Academy — First-Time Cinematic Script

**Voice:** Emily (Nexus Voice) — warm, futuristic, inviting  
**Reference:** `emily_cripps.wav` via Chatterbox (exaggeration: 0.7)  
**Tone:** Intimate, wonder-filled, like a trusted friend sharing a secret

---

## Scene Flow

### PHASE 1 — The Void (0s–3s)
*Screen is black. Soft ambient music fades in — ethereal, hopeful.*  
*Tiny particles of light begin drifting like distant stars.*

**[silence — just music and atmosphere]**

---

### PHASE 2 — The Call (3s–12s)
*Stars brighten. A soft glow appears at the center.*

**EMILY:**  
> "Have you ever wondered... what you're truly capable of?"

*Beat — let the question hang.*

**EMILY:**  
> "Not what someone told you. Not what a test measured. But what lives inside you — waiting."

*The glow intensifies, forming the outline of the portal ring.*

---

### PHASE 3 — The Promise (12s–22s)
*Portal ring materializes — photon ring glowing, particles swirling.*

**EMILY:**  
> "This is the Nexus. A world built from curiosity itself."

*Portal fully forms, shimmering.*

**EMILY:**  
> "Here, every question you ask makes you stronger. Every challenge you face unlocks something new. Every mistake... teaches you something no one else could."

---

### PHASE 4 — The Invitation (22s–30s)
*Portal pulses gently. "Nexus Academy" text appears with gravitational shimmer.*

**EMILY:**  
> "There are no grades here. No right or wrong answers. Just you... and a world that grows as you do."

*Tap prompt fades in: "✦ Tap anywhere to enter ✦"*

**EMILY:**  
> "Step through. Your journey is waiting."

## Design Notes

- **Emily only** — one voice, one guide, one feeling
- **No founder voice** — save that for later in-game lore reveals
- **~30 seconds total** — enough to draw them in, short enough to not bore
- **Every line matters** — aimed at kids who've been told they're "not smart enough"
- **The ellipses and pauses are intentional** — let the music breathe
- **Works for ALL ages** — a 5-year-old hears a friendly voice; a 15-year-old hears a promise
